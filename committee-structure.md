# 🎉 College Fest – Committee Structure  🎉
<p>The College Fest Committee is responsible for planning, organizing, and managing the college fest successfully. Each committee is assigned specific duties</p>


## 🤝Committee Structure

| S.No | Committee               | Main Responsibility                       |
|------ |-----------                 |---------------------                         |
| 1       | Event Coordinator  | Overall planning and coordination |
| 2       | Finance Committee | Budget and expense management |
| 3       | Cultural Committee| Cultural programs and performances |
| 4       | Technical Committee| Sound, lights, and technical support |
| 5       | Decoration Committee | Stage and venue decoration |
| 6       | Publicity Committee | Posters, invitations, and promotion |
| 7       | Registration Committee | Participant registration and attendance |
| 8       | Hospitality Committee | Guests, food, and refreshments |
| 9       | Discipline Committee | Crowd management and safety |
| 10     | Documentation Committee | Photos, videos, and event reports |<br>
